Since Day One

A Dream Born From Passion

Karma is more than a restaurant — it's a dream born from a passion for sharing the rich culinary heritage of India. Our founders, carrying generations of culinary expertise, set out to build a place in Jacksonville Beach where authentic Indian flavors could be savored in a vibrant, welcoming atmosphere.

From vegetarian and halal options to street snacks, thalis and refreshing drinks, we're dedicated to serving flavorful, comforting dishes that capture the true essence of Indian cuisine — the philosophy of Good Food, Good Mood, Good Karma.

Meet the Kitchen

Crafted By Hands That Care

Our kitchen team blends traditional recipes passed down through generations with a modern eye for presentation. Every curry is simmered low and slow; every tandoori dish is fired to order; every biryani is dum-cooked the authentic way.
